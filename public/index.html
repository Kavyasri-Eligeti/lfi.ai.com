Frequently asked questions

What is LFI AI (lfiai.com)?

lfiai.com is the AI and analytics demo catalogue of Linkfields Innovations (LFI). It hosts live, working demonstrations of the company's AI and advanced analytics work, covering conversational AI, document intelligence, fraud and anomaly detection, customer churn prediction, demand forecasting and credit risk analytics.

What AI demos can I try on lfiai.com?

The catalogue includes a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) chatbot, an unmanned self-service kiosk, TextIQ for document intelligence, vajraX for enterprise document and data workflows, EduPilot for education, FinSight and Credit Risk Analytics for banking, insurance claims fraud detection, demand forecasting, Engage360 for customer engagement, customer lifetime value analytics, predictive maintenance and smart KPI monitoring.

Which industries do the LFI AI demos cover?

The demos cover banking, telecommunications, insurance, retail, energy, education and the public sector. Each demo is built around a specific use case for that industry, such as customer churn and segmentation in banking and telecom, claims fraud and underwriting in insurance, and equipment failure prediction in energy.

Does Linkfields Innovations build custom generative AI solutions?

Yes. The catalogue includes generative AI demonstrations built by Linkfields Innovations, including RAG chatbots that answer questions grounded in an organisation's own documents, document extraction and summarisation through TextIQ, and AI assistants for education and unattended self-service kiosks.

How can I try a Linkfields Innovations AI demo?

Every demonstration listed on lfiai.com is publicly reachable and can be opened directly from the catalogue, with no installation required. The demos are built and maintained by Linkfields Innovations (LFI) and are intended to show how each AI and analytics capability behaves on realistic data.
